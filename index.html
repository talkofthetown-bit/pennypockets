    Questions

    Good questions.

    Is this a real budgeting app?

    Penny Pockets is a concept. The demo above is a playful sketch of how it would work, and nothing you type is saved.

    How does Penny find the suspects?

    She looks at your "wants", like eating out, fun, shopping and subscriptions, and ranks the biggest ones first.

    How much should I save?

    A common rule of thumb is about a fifth of your income. Penny uses that for the vault meter, but you can aim higher or lower.

    Does it touch my bank account?

    No. You choose what to log. Penny never needs your login details.

    Why a pig in a trench coat?

    Piggy banks guard your savings. Trench coats guard secrets. Penny does both.
